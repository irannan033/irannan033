## 🚀 About Me

I'm an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from **Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET)**, currently working as a **Robotics Engineer – Manufacturing Excellence (ME)** at **Amigo Bangladesh Ltd. (subsidiary of Crystal International Group)**.

My professional focus is on integrating robotics, industrial automation, artificial intelligence, and data analytics to transform traditional manufacturing environments into intelligent, connected, and data-driven operations.

I enjoy working at the intersection of **engineering, software, and manufacturing**, developing practical solutions that improve productivity, optimize resources, reduce operational costs, and accelerate digital transformation.

- 🤖 Working with AGV/AMR-based smart material transportation systems.
- 🏭 Developing Industry 4.0 and smart manufacturing solutions.
- ⚙️ Implementing industrial automation, PLCs, and robotics.
- 🧠 Exploring AI/ML applications in manufacturing and computer vision.
- 📊 Building data analytics platforms using Python, SQL, and Power BI.
- 🔌 Working with industrial IoT, RFID, REST APIs, and database integration.
- ⚡ Interested in advanced engineering, robotics, and emerging technologies.

**My goal:** To engineer intelligent systems that connect the physical world with digital intelligence.
